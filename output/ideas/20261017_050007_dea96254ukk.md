# [B] リノベーションにおける過剰投資の回避
## 適用条件
物件のリノベーションを行う際。
## 推奨アクション
投資した金額に対して、それに見合う価値（家賃収入や資産価値の上昇）が創出されるように、予算を厳格に管理する。
## ロジック・根拠
「So number two is overcapitalising. So again, what we want to do when we're looking at renovations is for every dollar you spend, is to try and get a dollar back. When you look at a project and you're looking at sort of what you can do there, it is quite easy to get carried away with various fixtures and fittings... But you've got to be very strict in your budget and what you're looking to do. Example, sort of going back to some of the renovation plays I've done, where we're going back to sort of where Sydney or Logan back in the day and you'd have sort of a bit of a rundown property but it's structurally sound. And you just, the wonders that a lick of paint and carpets could do to that particular property, uplift it both from a rental yield point of view and sort of capital growth point of view and extract equity. Was pretty game changing for us and we've applied it in our own sort of journey. And we kind of realized that any big sort of renovation opportunities where you've got to do, look at the kitchen or bathroom, you know, you're not necessarily going to get that value add back from it. So it's about making money at the end of the day. It's about improving your yield and being able to extract equity and hold for the long term. And so just going to be very strict in your budget and what you're looking to do here. So that's number two.」 (03:07 - 04:16)

# [B] リノベーションにおける「最良物件」の選択回避
## 適用条件
物件のリノベーションを行う際。
## 推奨アクション
その地域で最も立派な物件ではなく、リノベーションによって最も価値を向上させられる可能性のある物件（例：その通りで最も状態の悪い物件）を選択する。
## ロジック・根拠
「Picking the nicest property in the suburb. And I know that sounds a bit counterintuitive because you're going to do renovation uplift. But again, you're trying to find maybe the nicest looking property from the outside or the nicest street and buying one of the better houses on the street where there's not too much uplift that you can do there. Sounds counterintuitive but I've seen it before. You're much better off getting the worst house on the best street and seeing how you can add value there. It's the land, it's the value there and it's obviously the uplift that you can do there.」 (05:23 - 05:49)
## 前提・効かなくなる条件
その地域で「最良物件」が、リノベーションによっても大幅な価値向上が見込めないほど既に完成されている場合。
## 信頼度
中

# [B] リノベーションにおける「個人的嗜好」の排除
## 適用条件
物件のリノベーションを行う際。
## 推奨アクション
自身の個人的な好みに合わせてリノベーションするのではなく、ターゲットとする購入者層や賃借人が求めるであろうスタイルや仕様に合わせる。
## ロジック・根拠
「5. Renovating for personal taste. And so just because you like this particular property might be, you know, you've got a fancy for green splashbacks or whatever. It doesn't necessarily mean that the renter or the future buyer is going to like that particular property. So you're not trying to renovate it to your own taste. You're trying to do a renovation just that's going to, 95% of your market, most of the population, most of the people are going to get that property, they're going to be happy with it.」 (06:27 - 07:12)
